Projects

Two systems I designed and built end to end.

HandScript AI

Converts typed text into realistic handwriting, and can pick up a person's style from one photo of their writing.

  • Batched every line into a single TensorFlow call instead of one call per line: 20 lines went from 133 s to 17.6 s, 7.6× faster (measured on a laptop CPU).
  • Repeat requests are served from a SHA-256-keyed LRU cache in under 1 ms.
  • The model loads in a background thread, so the server starts at once and reports whether the model is still warming up or ready.
  • Two-stage style extraction: OpenCV segments the handwriting, then a PyTorch TRGAN model encodes the style.

NewsImpact

Reads financial news as it's published, estimates which stocks each story will move, and checks those calls against what prices actually did.

  • Pulls from RSS feeds, SEC EDGAR 8-K filings, GDELT and OCR of TV broadcasts, and maps each story to directly affected and second-order stocks.
  • Fine-tuned DistilRoBERTa to label central-bank language as hawkish or dovish: 76.7% accuracy overall, and 86.0% on the 57% of statements where it's confident enough to answer.
  • Tests every news-to-stock claim with an event study (abnormal return, t-statistic, p-value), and grades live calls at 1, 5 and 20 days.
  • Schedules sources with a UCB1 bandit, filters with Aho-Corasick matching and drops duplicates with SimHash, across about 16,000 lines of Python and TypeScript.
